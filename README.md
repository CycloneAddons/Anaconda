Anaconda is being brought back with a renewed vision. This repository will be the home of the project and its future development.

The direction of Anaconda will be shaped in part by community feedback. Suggestions and ideas are welcome, whether they're practical, ambitious, or entirely unconventional.

## Community Suggestions

Have an idea for Anaconda? Share it through the community or the project's GitHub repository. Suggestions are welcome across all areas, and no idea is too ambitious to put forward.

## Free to Use

Anaconda will be **100% free**.

- No premium plans.
- No paid feature tiers.
- No paywalls.
- No hidden charges.
